Rico Qi

Hi! 👋 I'm a master's student in the BS/MS program of University of Washington, studying Computer Science. I am advised by Abhishek Gupta as part of WEIRD Lab. My research focuses on VLAs, Sim-to-Real, and Real-World RL.

I joined Prof. Abhishek's lab in Jan 2025, where I worked with Sriyash Poddar on efficient learning from human interventions. We improved existing real world RL algorithms by 10-50% in success rate and 30% in effort. Then, I worked with Arhan Jain on post-training VLAs in simulation, where we post-trained a base VLA (pi-0.5) from 5% to 60% success rate without any real-world human demonstrations.

In Summer 2025, I was a modeling intern on the Tesla Foundation Models team, where I worked on RL for model improvement; I trained a SOTA model that was deployed at the Robotaxi Launch (Austin). I also competed at the ICPC World Finals twice.

Papers

AcceptedCoRL 2026

Q-OIL: Optimistic Exploration in Learning from Human Interventions

Pranav Teegavarapu, Sriyash Poddar, Rico Qi, Cleah Winston, Krisha Khandelwal, Natasha Jaques, Abhishek Gupta

We design an RL algorithm that use interventions as optimism signals for exploration. Attains 10-50% higher success rate with 30% less interventions against SOTA baselines HIL-SERL and HG-DAgger, across real tasks and users.

In submissionCoRL 2026, Sim2Real2Field

Improving Generalist Policies with Simulation Post-Training

Arhan Jain*, Rico Qi*, Gavin Angeloff, Aykut Onol, Paarth Shah, Abhishek Gupta

We design a VLA post-training pipeline that requires no human teleoperation effort, by leveraging simulation. We successfully improve pi-0.5 from 5% to 60% success rate on long-horizon precision tasks, beating baselines that require human demos.
